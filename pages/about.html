Who I Am

About Me

I'm Mahammad Alakbarli, a third-year Computer Engineering student at Karabakh University and a Machine Learning Engineer at Chevir, where I work on translating Azerbaijani Sign Language into text and back. I treat programming as a way to solve real problems rather than as coursework, so most of my time goes into things people can actually use — an adaptive learning platform for schoolchildren, autonomy software that placed 3rd at the Turkic World UAV Competition, and security tooling such as a CVE triage CLI. Alongside that, I lead the security and cryptography track at Azeros. Holberton School's project-based programme, which I joined on a full scholarship, taught me to turn theory into working software and to work through hard problems on my own. I care about readable code, clear structure and careful handling of sensitive data, and I see continuous learning as part of the job.
